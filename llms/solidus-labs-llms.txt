# Solidus Labs


> Solidus Labs is the category-defining platform for crypto-native compliance, trade surveillance, and risk monitoring. Founded in 2018 by Goldman Sachs veterans, the company provides the HALO platform, an AI-powered compliance control hub trusted by leading exchanges, broker-dealers, market makers, and financial institutions globally. Solidus covers digital assets, derivatives, equities, and fixed income from a single platform.


Solidus Labs operates eight product modules under the HALO platform: Trade Surveillance, Transaction Monitoring, Stablecoin Monitoring, Token Monitoring (powered by TokenSniffer), Execution Quality, Case Management, Agentic-AI Workflows, and Professional Services. The company serves clients across six continents including regulated exchanges, broker-dealers (FINRA-registered and international), market makers, asset managers, custodians, OTC desks, stablecoin issuers, and regulators.


Key differentiators: crypto-native detection models purpose-built for volatile, fragmented, 24/7 digital asset markets; multidimensional architecture correlating trading behavior with funding flows, KYC data, wallet analytics, and social sentiment; unified fiat and onchain monitoring in a single engine; integration with Chainalysis, TRM Labs, and Elliptic; and the industry's first execution quality framework for digital assets.


Headquartered in New York, with offices in Tel Aviv and London. CEO: Asaf Meir. 
## Products


- [HALO Platform](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/platform): Unified compliance control hub. Eight integrated modules with shared risk intelligence, multidimensional data architecture, and configurable detection across every asset class and venue type.
- [Trade Surveillance](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/trade-surveillance): Real-time, cross-venue trade surveillance for digital assets and traditional markets. Detects spoofing, wash trading, layering, front-running, insider trading, and cross-venue manipulation with crypto-native behavioral models.
- [Transaction Monitoring](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/transaction-monitoring): Unified fiat and onchain AML screening. Screens deposits, withdrawals, and transactional flows across fiat rails and crypto in one engine with multidimensional risk scoring, onchain analytics, and continuous sanctions screening.
- [Stablecoin Monitoring](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/stablecoin-monitoring): Full-lifecycle compliance for stablecoin issuers covering primary market operations (mint/burn, reserve monitoring) and secondary market activity (onchain circulation, DeFi interactions). Supports GENIUS Act, MiCA, and HKMA requirements.
- [Token Monitoring](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/token-monitoring): Powered by TokenSniffer. Automated smart contract scam detection, liquidity and rug pull risk assessment, holder distribution analysis, and real-time malicious token alerting across 15+ chains. 250,000+ scam tokens identified since 2021.
- [Execution Quality](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/execution-quality): The industry's first execution quality framework for digital asset markets. Cross-venue price benchmarking, execution deviation monitoring, and defensible audit trails. Supports MiCA Article 78, FINRA Rule 5310, MAS DPT Framework, and HK SFC guidelines.
- [Case Management](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/case-management): AI-powered investigation hub unifying trade surveillance, transaction monitoring, and KYC alerts in one console. Multidimensional case prioritization, automated triage, shared risk metadata, and full audit trail from alert to regulatory filing.
- [Agentic-AI Workflows](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/agentic-based-compliance): AI agent network for compliance operations. Solomon (orchestration agent) coordinates specialized agents for alert triage, investigation drafting, regulatory research, and cross-module correlation.
- [Professional Services](https://www.soliduslabs.com/solutions/professional-services): Investigative services (market abuse, insider trading, smart contract forensics), compliance program development, cross-jurisdictional licensing support, and HALO optimization including model tuning, analyst training, and policy development.
